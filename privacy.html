Hira Kitchenato - Privacy Policy

Applies to the Hira Kitchenato Customer app and the Hira Kitchenato Restaurant (staff) app

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Hira Kitchenato ("we", "us", "our") is a restaurant based in Bahawalpur, Pakistan. This policy explains what information our mobile apps collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using our apps you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Customer app

  • Name and phone number - to identify your orders and to verify your phone number with a one-time code (OTP) sent by SMS.
  • Delivery address - the address you enter so we can deliver your order.
  • Location (GPS) - with your permission, your device location while you use the app, to help you set your delivery location on the map. You can refuse or turn off location permission at any time in your phone settings; you can still type your address manually.
  • Order history - the items, amounts, status and time of the orders you place.
  • Notification token - a technical identifier of your device, used to send you order updates and offers as push notifications.
  • Technical and crash information - device model, operating system version, app version and crash reports, used to fix bugs. The app may also create an anonymous account identifier so it can work before you sign in.

Restaurant (staff) app

  • Staff login details (email address and password handled by our sign-in provider), a notification token so staff receive new-order alerts, and the order information shown to staff.

We do not collect your payment card or bank details in the apps. Orders are paid in cash on delivery or by manual bank/mobile-wallet transfer outside the app.

2. How we use your information

  • To receive, prepare and deliver your orders and to contact you about them.
  • To verify your phone number and keep your account secure.
  • To show your past orders.
  • To send order updates and, if you allow notifications, offers and announcements.
  • To find and fix errors and improve the apps.

3. Who can see your information

Your orders, name, phone number and delivery address are visible to Hira Kitchenato staff and delivery personnel only as needed to fulfil your order. We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for advertising.

The apps use Google Firebase services (authentication, database, cloud messaging, crash reporting and hosting) to operate. Google processes data on our behalf under its own privacy terms: firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We may also disclose information if required by law.

4. Notifications

Push notifications are optional. You can switch them off at any time in your phone's Settings under Notifications.

5. How long we keep information

We keep order and account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to run our business and meet legal obligations. When you ask us to delete your data (see below), we delete or anonymise your personal information, except records we must keep by law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access or correct the information we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and data - send us a request using the contact details below from the phone number registered in the app, and we will delete your data within 30 days.
  • Withdraw permissions (location, notifications) at any time in your phone settings.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted connections and access controls, to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
