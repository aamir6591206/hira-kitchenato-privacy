Common questions

I did not receive my verification (OTP) code.
Check that the phone number is correct and has network signal, wait a minute and try again. If it still fails, call or email us.

How do I pay for my order?
Pay cash on delivery or by manual bank/mobile-wallet transfer as shown in the app. The app does not take card payments.

How do I delete my account and data?
Contact us from your registered phone number using the details above. We delete your data within 30 days. See our Privacy Policy.

My order has a problem.
Call us with your name and the time of the order and we will fix it.
